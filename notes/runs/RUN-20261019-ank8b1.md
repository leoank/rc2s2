## Notes

Plan step 2 of [[EXP-20261019-ayya0c]]: `bench.py` builds the hub's `events` table in SQLite 3.53.3
(Python 3.14.7's `sqlite3`, not the hub's sqlx; the B-tree work is SQLite's either way) on
this machine (Apple silicon, internal SSD), set as the hub opens it: WAL, synchronous FULL,
a 2 MB page cache. Four variants, each grown to 10 million synthetic lines in transactions
of 1,000: no uuid index; a unique (node, session, uuid) index with the uuid as text; the
same with a 64 MB cache; the uuid as 16 bytes. Sessions change every 180 lines and uuids
are random v4, as in [[RUN-20261019-an0gmm]]. Payloads are a fixed 200 bytes rather than the
7.6 KB real mean, to keep each database near 6 GB; that understates the main table, not
the index.

Result (`RUN-20261019-ank8b1/bench.txt`), at 10 million rows:

| variant | batched inserts/s | single-event transactions/s | replay adds | bytes/row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 126,781 | 14,221 | (no index) | 493 |
| text | 82,759 | 10,221 | 0 at 15,121/s | 665 |
| text, 64 MB cache | 76,383 | 9,034 | 0 at 15,367/s | 665 |
| blob | 83,787 | 9,407 | 0 at 16,647/s | 615 |

- With the text index, batched inserts start at 107,000 a second and fall to 83,000 at 10
  million; without it, from 150,000 to 127,000. A gradual slope, no cliff.
- Single-event transactions, how the hub stores today, keep 72% of the rate without the
  index, ten times the 1,000 a second set as the bar.
- The index and the column cost 172 bytes a row, 2.2% of a line at this machine's mean
  size.
- A larger page cache does not help (the OS file cache already holds the index), and the
  16-byte uuid saves 50 bytes a row while costing a conversion; text stays.
